About TickStack

A calmer way to make progress.

Built for Daily momentum Small actions, steady rhythm, meaningful results.

TickStack is a minimalist habit timer designed to help people turn scattered intentions into a repeatable routine. It turns your day into a sequence of small, visible wins that feel manageable enough to begin.

Why it exists

Most productivity tools either overwhelm you with complexity or ask for too much willpower before you even start. TickStack was built to solve that exact problem by giving you a simple ritual: choose what matters, assign a time, and move through it one step at a time.

The idea is not to perfect your day. It is to lower the barrier to action. A 20-second hydration check, a 60-second stretch, a quick desk reset — each one is small enough to do without negotiation and meaningful enough to compound over time.

When the routine is visible and timed, it becomes easier to follow through. The app turns intention into motion, and motion into consistency.

Principles

01 / Simple Uncluttered by design

Every interaction is focused on helping you act without friction.

02 / Human Made for real routines

It supports the messy reality of busy mornings, desk days, and recovery breaks.

03 / Consistent Built to repeat

Progress comes from steady repetition, not dramatic bursts of productivity.
